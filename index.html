Say it out loud

Tap the microphone and speak. Tap again to stop. Your words appear below so you can fix anything before saving.

This browser can't do voice-to-text. Open your brain in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android. (Safari works on some iPhones but not all.) You can still type in the box below.

In Chrome, your browser sends the audio to Google to turn it into text. Nothing goes into your brain until you press Save.

Capture a YouTube video

Paste a video link. Your capture-youtube server function fetches the transcript for you.

Or paste the transcript yourself
  1. Click Get Transcript below — the video opens in a new tab.
  2. Under the video, click the description (…more), scroll down and click Show transcript.
  3. The transcript opens on the right. Click and drag from the first line to the last to select it all, then copy (Cmd+C / Ctrl+C).
  4. Come back here and paste it in the box below. Timestamps are removed for you.
